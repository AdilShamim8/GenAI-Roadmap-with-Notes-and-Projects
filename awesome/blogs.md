# Blogs

> Verified 2026-10-20. Listed roughly in order of signal-to-noise.

## Must-follow

- [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/) — Daily, dense, essential. Especially his prompt-injection coverage.
- [Chip Huyen](https://huyenchip.com/) — Less frequent; always worth reading.
- [Hamel Husain](https://hamel.dev/) — Evals-first practitioner.
- [Interconnects (Nathan Lambert)](https://www.interconnects.ai/) — The best weekly read on RL post-training, open models, and lab strategy.
- [Anthropic blog](https://www.anthropic.com/news) — Major model releases + engineering posts.
- [OpenAI news + cookbook](https://openai.com/news/) — Skip the marketing; read the engineering. Cookbook code lives at [developers.openai.com/cookbook](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook).
- [Google DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/) — Research-heavy.

## Worth-following

- [Jay Alammar](https://jalammar.github.io/) — Visual transformers/LLM explainers.
- [Eugene Yan](https://eugeneyan.com/) — Applied ML/LLM patterns.
- [Lilian Weng](https://lilianweng.github.io/) — Long-form technical posts.
- [Daniel Ehrenberg](https://leoo.dev/) — Tooling/standards perspective.
- [Ahead of AI (Sebastian Raschka)](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/) — Research explained for practitioners.
- [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/) — Data-driven trend analysis on compute, scaling, and the AI economy.

## Engineering blogs from companies

- [HuggingFace blog](https://huggingface.co/blog) — Open-weight ecosystem.
- [Modal blog](https://modal.com/blog) — Inference infrastructure.
- [Replicate blog](https://replicate.com/blog) — Practical patterns.
