# Job Market Trends (2026)

> **Last verified:** 2026-10-20

## What's hot

- **Agent engineers.** Anyone who can ship a multi-agent system that works in production.
- **Evals engineers.** Rare; very valuable. Be the person who knows how to measure LLM quality.
- **AI infra / platform.** Building internal platforms for other teams to ship AI products.
- **Forward-deployed engineers (FDE).** Travel to customer, ship custom AI. Anthropic and OpenAI both hire this role heavily.

## What's cooled

- **Pure prompt engineers.** "I write prompts" is no longer a job.
- **ML engineers without LLM experience.** Strong market, but premiums are shifting to LLM-fluent candidates.
- **GenAI at non-AI companies.** Many "AI strategy" roles in 2024 were headcount-driven; budgets tightening in 2026.

## What's emerging

- **Voice / realtime engineers.** Realtime API skills are scarce.
- **Safety / red-team engineers.** Regulation-driven demand (EU AI Act, sectoral rules).
- **AI cost engineers.** Companies that shipped in 2024 are now optimizing their bills in 2026.

## Company trends

- **Frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind):** Hard to get into; very high comp; research-leaning.
- **Application-layer startups:** Easiest entry point; high learning velocity; equity is a lottery ticket.
- **Big tech (Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple):** Stable; comp competitive; less breadth.
- **Consultancies (Deloitte, McKinsey, BCG):** AI consulting is booming; great for generalists; lots of travel.

## Geographic trends

- **SF Bay:** Still the center. Most roles; highest comp; most competition.
- **NYC:** Strong; finance + AI is a hot niche.
- **Seattle / Austin / Denver:** Strong big-tech presence.
- **London:** EU leader; frontier labs have offices.
- **Toronto / Montreal:** DeepMind, Cohere, academic pipeline.
- **Bangalore:** Strong startup ecosystem; lower comp but high growth.
- **Remote:** More common in 2026 than 2024; still a minority of roles.

## The macro picture

- AI engineer demand exceeded supply through 2024–2025.
- 2026 is cooling slightly as companies digest 2024 hiring sprees.
- Quality bar is rising: 2023 "I used ChatGPT" hireability is gone.
- Long-term outlook: strong. The infrastructure doesn't exist yet for the agent-heavy applications companies want to build.

## What this means for you

- **If you're junior:** Get to "production multi-agent" as fast as possible. That's the bar.
- **If you're mid:** Specialize. Evals, agent orchestration, realtime — pick one.
- **If you're senior:** Build a public portfolio. The market rewards visible expertise disproportionately.
- **If you're staff+:** The market is yours. Multiple offers are common.
