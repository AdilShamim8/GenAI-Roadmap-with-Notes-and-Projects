# Salary Bands (2026)

> **Last verified:** 2026-10-20 · Sources: Levels.fyi, Built In, Glassdoor, Robert Half 2026 Salary Guide, KORE1 placement data (all US, all USD).
> Salary sources disagree by design: Levels.fyi skews to Big Tech (median base ~$211K), national medians run ~$170-185K, and signed offers for genuine production-AI work cluster higher. Treat the ranges below as planning bands, not guarantees.

## US bands

| Level | Years exp | Base salary | Total comp (base + equity + bonus) | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------------|-------------------------------------|-------|
| Junior AI eng | 0–2 | $90K–$135K | $110K–$160K | CS/MS degree + real project work is the norm, not the exception |
| Mid AI eng | 3–5 | $140K–$210K | $170K–$260K | Strongest demand band |
| Senior AI eng | 6–9 | $180K–$280K | $220K–$350K+ | Production agents, evals, cost engineering |
| Staff AI eng | 10+ | $250K–$400K+ | $350K–$600K+ | Cross-team architecture |
| Principal+ AI eng | 12+ | $300K+ | $450K–$1M+ | Sets AI direction; top of band is frontier-lab territory |

**The frontier-lab premium.** A small cohort (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and hot research startups) pays far above these bands — senior packages at $700K+ total comp, and Levels.fyi's 2026 median *total comp* for an OpenAI engineer is ~$795K. At these levels, equity marks to secondary-sale prices and is genuinely illiquid.

## Equity reality

- **Public company:** Equity is liquid; assume 4-year vest, 1-year cliff.
- **Late-stage startup (Series C+):** Some liquidity via tender offers; otherwise illiquid.
- **Early-stage:** Treat as a lottery ticket. Negotiate cash up.
- **AI-specific:** Watch valuation discipline; the 2023–24 cohort of AI startups marked at 100× ARR has been repricing through 2025–26. The 2026 funding cycle rewards revenue over narrative.

## Negotiation

- **Always negotiate.** First offer is rarely best.
- **Multiple offers are leverage.** Even one competing offer moves comp 10–30%.
- **Equity is negotiable too.** Especially at startups.
- **Sign-on bonus is a one-time lever.** Use it to bridge a base gap.
- **Don't accept "we don't negotiate."** Almost always false.

## Contracting / consulting

- **Independent AI eng day rate (US):** $800–$2,500/day, tied to specialization; inference-optimization and multi-agent experience command the top of the range.
- **Long-term engagement (3+ months):** Lower day rate, more stability.
- **Equity-only:** Almost never worth it.
